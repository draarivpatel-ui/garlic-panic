How I Built This - Dev Notes by Aariv Patel Architecture & Godot 4 Sync

1. Global State Machine & Speed Ramp

In both scripts/global.gd and this web build, a central Global state machine tracks your 5 garlic lives, current round (1 to 12), streak, and speed_multiplier. Every microgame win raises the speed multiplier by +0.15x up to 2.5x, shrinking the microgame countdown timer (7.0s / speed) while accelerating hazard speeds.

2. Hand-Written Vector Sprites & Web Audio

Instead of downloading external sprite packs, I wrote the garlic bulb, falling spikes, golden star, and chef cleaver directly as clean SVG/Canvas geometry (assets/garlic.svg, spike.svg, star.svg). All retro sound effects (win arpeggios, fail buzzes, mash pops, and cleaver chops) are synthesized live via the browser's AudioContext.

3. Why Two Runtimes (Godot 4 + Canvas)?

I built the core project in Godot 4.7 with GDScript scenes (minigame_1.tscn through minigame_6.tscn) and headless test harnesses, then mirrored the exact 640x360 scene graph into this single-file HTML5 Canvas port so anyone can play all 6 microgames or practice individual stages right in the browser with zero loading screens.
