GARLIC PANIC!

Godot 4.7 + HTML5 Web Build 4 Microgames
Godot 4 Source Repo
Controls: Left / Right or A / D = Move SPACE or Click = Mash / Lock In
Jump to Microgame:

1. DODGE! Minigame 1

Move your garlic left and right with Arrow keys or A/D to survive falling crimson spikes until the timer hits 0.0s.

2. MASH! Minigame 2

Mash SPACE or rapidly click the giant garlic bulb to hit the target quota before time runs out. Quota scales with speed!

3. CATCH! Minigame 3

Slide the golden paddle left and right to catch the falling star before it hits the dungeon floor.

4. TIMING! Minigame 4

Wait for the oscillating cursor to enter the green target zone and press SPACE or click LOCK IN to nail the timing!
